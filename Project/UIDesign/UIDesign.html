Professional and Concept Work

First and Foremost...

These designs are part of a growing library of experiments and ideas that I've had or work that I've done during for other companies. These designs are potential design solutions for existing problems. This collection is documentation of my growth as a product designer and as a person.

Loket Castle Showcase

I really wanted a way to show off my photography so what better way than to make a dashboard / landing page on the Loket Castle for tourists. Basic information such as the weather and quick description of the castle is enough for most while subjects such as architecture and history are more for the curious

Canada Life Advisor Hub

The purpose behind the page was to provide a single point of contact for insurance advisors, field managers and whole sales to access information about the master brand initiative in the form of an advisor hub. This is a place where advisors can come to download information on the Canada Life rebrand to deliver to their clients and improve the user experience for advisors for getting their questions answered efficiently

Takai Food Price Tracking UI

A concept Food price tracking interface displaying information from price history, supermarket vendor and price records for the selected product. The intended use is for users to search a product they wish to purchase and to verify or price match that the price they’re getting from the supermarket is the best one

Portfolio Landing Page

Testing out a concept photography portfolio landing page for a basic webpage. Very basic, eye catching and to the point landing page for most visitors. Important links to photos and previous experience logs on such as weddings, portrait shoot and contracted events

Canada Life Form Design

As part of Canada Life’s new rebranding move, insurance advisors and plan sponsors are able to have their questions quickly addressed. A new form UI was required and would live on the contact us section of the Canada Life website.
