Challenge

Trading is a competitive landscape

Where people choose to hold, spend and trade their money are all significant decisions that many do not take lightly. The modern fintech landscape has drastically changed over the past few years giving users a wide variety of options such as Robinhood, Webull, Moo moo and IBKR to name a few.

The bar is constantly raised where it is a standard that any trading platform must be efficient, customizable and handle the information density required for modern trading. The challenge for our team was to not only re-design a complex ecosystem but to also build a competitive platform that will stand the test of time.

Solution

A workspace that adapts to you

Modular workspace architecture

We designed a modular panel system that lets traders arrange their workspace exactly how they want. Each panel — charts, watchlists, order entry, news — can be resized, repositioned, and saved as custom layouts.

[Image placeholder] Modular workspace — custom layout

Traders can drag, resize, and save custom panel layouts

Core Feature

Smart Watchlist

Context-aware watchlist that surfaces relevant data based on what the trader is doing — showing options data when analyzing options, news when scanning, and execution shortcuts when ready to trade.

[Image placeholder] Smart Watchlist UI

Core Feature

One-click order entry

Reduced the order placement flow from 5 clicks to 1. Power users can execute directly from charts, watchlists, or the keyboard with configurable hotkeys.
